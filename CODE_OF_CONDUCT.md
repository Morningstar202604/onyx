# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our pledge

为了营造开放、友好的社区环境，Onyx 的维护者与贡献者承诺：无论年龄、体型、身体能力、民族、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向，参与本项目的人都享有不受骚扰的体验。

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, Onyx maintainers and contributors pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 我们的标准 / Our standards

有助于创造积极环境的行为包括（Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include）：

- 使用友好、包容的语言（using welcoming and inclusive language）；
- 尊重不同的观点与经验（being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences）；
- 优雅地接受建设性批评（gracefully accepting constructive criticism）；
- 关注什么对社区最有利（focusing on what is best for the community）；
- 对其他成员表达同理心（showing empathy toward other community members）。

不可接受的行为包括（Examples of unacceptable behavior include）：

- 性暗示语言/图像与不受欢迎的性关注（sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention）；
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损性评论以及人身或政治攻击（trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks）；
- 公开或私下骚扰（public or private harassment）；
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息（publishing others' private information without explicit permission）；
- 其他在专业环境中可合理认定为不适当的行为（other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting）。

## 我们的职责 / Our responsibilities

维护者负责澄清并执行行为标准，并对任何不可接受的行为采取恰当且公平的纠正措施。

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior and take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本行为准则适用于所有项目空间，也适用于个人在公共空间代表项目或社区的情形（如使用官方邮箱、官方社交媒体账号，或在线上/线下活动中作为代表）。

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community (e.g., using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event).

## 执行 / Enforcement

可通过仓库 About 页面提供的维护者联系方式报告滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为。所有投诉都会得到审查与调查，并会作出必要且适当的回应。

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers via the contact info in the repository's About section. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
